Current Projects

Customer Segmentation with K-Means

This course project explores customer groups in a dataset of 200 mall customers. I standardized the data, selected five clusters, and evaluated the results with three clustering metrics. The outcome was a notebook, report, and presentation describing the customer segments.

Local AI Models for Interior Design

This project compares two local language models on interior design and commercial furniture tasks. I tested Llama 3.2 3B and Qwen3 4B with seven prompts and evaluated quality, speed, memory use, and unsupported claims. Qwen performed better on the tested quality criteria, while Llama used fewer resources.

Interior Design Prompt Library

This project organizes reusable prompts for interior design and office furniture tasks. I developed the prompts and evaluated their outputs for accuracy, clarity, completeness, and other practical criteria. The result was a prompt library with an evaluation table that helped identify where the responses needed improvement.
